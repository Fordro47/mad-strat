### Color Guidance For AI

- Use Vanilla Creme as the dominant background color.
- Use Charcoal Ink for primary text and serious grounding.
- Use Midnight Plum for depth, headers, panels, or premium brand moments.
- Use Bordeaux Wine sparingly as a signature accent.
- Use Pastel Blue for contrast, refinement, and subtle visual relief.
- Avoid neon colors, high-saturation brights, playful pastels, and harsh pure black unless technically required.

## Typography System

### Heading Font

| Font | Uses | Brand Feeling |

| Trajan Pro | Headlines, logo typography, premium branding, print collateral | Institutional, timeless, classical, authoritative |

### Body Font

| Font | Uses | Brand Feeling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Montserrat | Website copy, presentations, reports, social media, marketing materials | Clean, modern, strategic, professional |

### Typography Guidance For AI

- Pair classical serif-style headings with clean modern body text.
- Favor generous spacing, refined hierarchy, and editorial restraint.
- Avoid novelty fonts, overly rounded fonts, tech-heavy fonts, or childish handwritten styles.
- When Trajan Pro is unavailable, use a classical serif or Roman-inspired display font as a substitute.
- When Montserrat is unavailable, use a clean geometric sans-serif as a substitute.

## Do And Do Not

### Do

- Use cream, plum, bordeaux, charcoal, and pastel blue.
- Use refined editorial layouts.
- Reference policy, strategy, authorship, leadership, and influence.
- Keep copy concise, composed, and confident.
- Favor timeless institutional visuals.
- Preserve the tagline exactly: "Principled Policy. Strategic Solutions."

### Do Not

- Make the brand feel playful, trendy, loud, or tech-startup oriented.
- Use generic patriotic imagery unless it is subtle and editorial.
- Overuse flags, stars, eagles, Capitol silhouettes, or campaign-style motifs.
- Make the feminine elements overly floral, delicate, or decorative.
- Use casual slang, hype language, or hard-sell marketing copy.
- Dilute the brand into generic consulting language.
